### TICKET-053 — Floor Splotch Flickering / Swimming Pool Effect (Lumen GI)

- Notes:
  - **Root cause:** Lumen's temporal GI probe system causes swimming-pool caustic light patterns. NOT caused by emissive materials, light fixtures, or ISM batcher specifically.
  - **Fix (session 39):** PPV_GlobalDarkness (unbound, priority 10) disables Lumen GI entirely:
    - `dynamic_global_illumination_method = NONE`

    - `indirect_lighting_intensity = 0.0025` (tuned brightness — horror darkness with subtle fill)
  - **Brightness control:** `indirect_lighting_intensity` is the working knob. `auto_exposure_bias` had no effect on editor viewport. Scale: 0.0=black, 0.0025=good horror, 0.1=way too bright.
  - **UE5 Python property name:** `override_dynamic_global_illumination_method` (NOT `_type`)
  - **Scripts:** `_fix_ppv_no_lumen.py` (disables Lumen), `_bump_light.py` (brightness tuning), `_check_ppv2.py` (diagnostic)
  - **Side effects:** All interior SpotLight/RectLight actors + light fixture meshes were deleted during troubleshooting (incorrect root cause). May need partial restoration later.
