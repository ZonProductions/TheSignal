# SIGNAL — Polaris: Org Background

*Companion to `signal_story_bible.md` and `campaign_structure.md`. Who Polaris is, why
they were sitting on the rift, and how that lore reaches the player. Polaris is **not the
antagonist** — the void is. This doc is the org's philosophy and the world it built; it's
delivered almost entirely through **found documents** (notes, logs, printed emails), never
cutscenes.*

---

## The shape — Shinra, not Umbrella

Polaris is **Shinra-shaped** (FF7): a legitimate science-and-technology company on the
billboard, and an inner circle chasing the impossible in the back rooms. The public face
and the secret are the **same domain — biology.** Like Shinra's mako *was* refined
lifestream, Polaris's miracle medicine is quietly **rift-derived biology**, and the void is
biology from the other side. They all rhyme.

Two reads we explicitly rejected:
- **Not Black Mesa / Umbrella.** Their hubris is not the *trigger* of the disaster. The
  breach is a containment **failure / sabotage**, not "they poked it too hard on camera."
  Polaris's ambition makes them *vulnerable*; it isn't the on-screen cause.
- **Not a government agency.** Polaris is **private** — no flag, no oversight, no public
  accountability. (FBC's clearance-and-redaction *culture* is borrowed; its federal
  ownership is not.)

---

## Contained, not created

Polaris **did not make the rift.** They found/inherited it and held it shut. The shielded
containment room, the breach protocols, the security tiers, and the failsafe all exist
because **Polaris knew** what they were sitting on. Containment isn't their mission — it's
the **cost of access.** They keep the door shut so they don't lose the thing they're
studying.

---

## The front (Tier 2) — what the world sees

The **world's leading biotech company.** Cures, longevity, crop yield — miracle medicine
that ended diseases it shouldn't have been able to. Prestigious, well-funded, the reason a
whole remote campus-town exists. This is the register of the **surface documents** the
player finds early: proud frontier science, funding justifications, peer-review bravado,
"greatest discovery in history."

**Why biotech earns its keep in-game:**
- **Greenery / bio-labs.** Greenhouses, vertical farms, seed vaults, hydroponics, specimen
  storage — environmental variety you can't get from grey labs, with a built-in horror
  contrast: sterile white grow-room → the same room overgrown and void-choked.
- **Plantmass void creatures.** Gives the *non-humanoid void spawn* a hard origin: the void
  doesn't only hollow people, it hits Polaris's **biomass** — cultures, plants, specimens —
  and grows new things from it. Vegetal body horror, distinct from the humanoid necromorphs.
  Touchstones: TLOU cordyceps, *Annihilation*, RE's Plant 42.

---

## The secret (Tier 4) — what the inner circle is really after

Beneath the medicine, the leadership is reaching for the impossible: **the far side, and
beating death.** A biotech front whose true believers chase transcendence points straight
at the body and mortality — post-human, immortality, what's *through* the door. This is the
register of the **deep / restricted / founder's-private documents**: the realization that
what's behind the rift isn't a phenomenon to catalog but something **vast and old that was
never meant to be opened**, and the void is only its edge.

**Delivery = a document gradient.** The prose gets bigger and worse the deeper Marcus goes
— which *is* the "climb toward the source" structure. Surface clearance → Tier 2 register;
high clearance / founder's private logs → Tier 4 register. The security-tier ladder already
in the campaign gates the escalation for free.

**Tone guardrail:** Tier 4 stays **clinical and oblique**, never grand. "We gazed upon the
infinite" is the corn the bible bans. Imply the scale; never narrate it.

---

## How wide the secret is

**An inner circle over an innocent majority.** Thousands of ordinary biologists, doctors,
staff and their families lived on top of a secret a handful of true believers kept. The
rank-and-file thought they were curing disease — they were the *front*, unwitting. This
keeps Polaris human (not cartoon-evil) and makes the breach genuinely tragic: the people it
kills had no idea what they were guarding.

---

## Culture & philosophy

A **private cult of genius** built around a founder's hunger: answerable to no government
and no shareholders, only "the work." Operationally it runs like a **priesthood guarding a
sacred thing** — clearance levels, redaction, need-to-know — *keepers and seekers* at once.
The secrecy and the self-contained campus-town are the same instinct: hold the sacred thing
close, let no one outside know it exists.

**Real-world anchor:** Los Alamos — a secret science town where staff and families lived
on-site, need-to-know, built around something that could level a region. Polaris with the
serial numbers filed off.

**The failsafe nuke** (ending A): even true believers hedged. An org this committed to the
prize built exactly *one* safety — if it all goes wrong, erase everything. No government to
call for cleanup, so they made their own scorched earth. The single moment caution beat
ambition.

## The deep file on Marcus

Polaris keeps a **deep file** on its people — Marcus included. In-fiction, this is the
Guide's cover for knowing Marcus's past: the player rationalizes it as *"he's got my HR
file, he's been watching."*

But mind the **seam**: a file holds the **surface** — his record, the family trouble, why
he took a job this far from home. It **can't** hold the intimate fragments — the exact words
said the night his dad fell. So the file is the lie the player swallows early; the moment
the whispers get *too specific for any file* is the moment the real source bleeds through.
**File = cover, infection = truth, the gap between them = the clue.** (The void reaches
Marcus through the slow infection in him — see `marcus_backstory.md` / the tracker + infection
notes in the bible.)

## Open knobs (parked)

- **The founder** — name, the personal root of the obsession (what's beyond death? someone
  he lost?). Carries the Tier 4 private logs.
- **Plantmass creature origin** — **made by the breach** (void poured into the greenhouses
  that night) vs **already growing** (Polaris was unknowingly cultivating rift-biology for
  years; the breach just *woke* it). Lean: the second — darker, more Shinra, the monsters
  were always Polaris's. Not locked.
- **The scientist on the far side** (`campaign_structure.md`) — her tie to Polaris and the
  containment program; how much of this background she voices before the choice.
- **What the front product literally is** — the specific miracle drug/line, if it's ever
  named on a label/log.
